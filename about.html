About SanskritParichay

SanskritParichay is a playful Sanskrit adventure for children and families. Mithu guides learners through magical worlds, mini-games, stars, and joyful practice.

This site does not collect child names, emails, or analytics. It stores progress in your browser only, so progress does not sync across devices.

Built as a cheerful, privacy-first learning space for kids.

How this helps children learn

The activities are designed to be simple, playful, and repeatable, so children can build confidence with Sanskrit sounds step by step.

Parents and teachers can use it as a friendly practice companion at home.
