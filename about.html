About SanskritParichay

SanskritParichay is a free learning project for children and families. The first world is Alphabet Forest, designed as a playful journey with audio support, mini-games, stars, retries, and local progress.

This site does not collect child names, emails, or analytics. It stores progress in your browser only, so progress does not sync across devices.

Learning content is intentionally structured for review. Final language verification should be done by a Sanskrit teacher before broad release.

Built as a playful, privacy-first learning space for kids.

How this helps children learn

The activities are designed to be simple, playful, and repeatable, so children can build confidence with Sanskrit sounds step by step.

Parents and teachers can use it as a friendly practice companion at home.
